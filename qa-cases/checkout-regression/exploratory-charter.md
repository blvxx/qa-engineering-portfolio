# Exploratory Charter — Checkout State & Pricing

**Timebox:** 45 minutes  
**Mission:** Find inconsistencies in cart totals or checkout state after user actions.

## Areas to explore
- Apply promo, change quantity, remove item, switch shipping, then restore item.
- Rapidly click plus/minus and submit.
- Refresh or navigate back at cart, checkout, and confirmation.
- Use whitespace, mixed case, long strings, and pasted values in promo/email fields.
- Attempt zero, negative, decimal, very large quantities, and quantity beyond stock.
- Check mobile viewport and keyboard focus.
